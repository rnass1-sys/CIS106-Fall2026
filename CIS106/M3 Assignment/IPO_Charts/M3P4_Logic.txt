[INPUT]
* Vehicle make
* Vehicle model
* Original MSRP amount
* Discount percent (entered as a decimal)

[PROCESSING]
1. Prompt the user for car make, model, MSRP, and discount.
2. Multiply the MSRP by the discount percent to find the savings.
   Formula: amount_off = msrp * discount_percent
3. Subtract the savings from the original MSRP to find the final price.
   Formula: discounted_price = msrp - amount_off

[OUTPUT]
* Vehicle make
* Vehicle model
* Original MSRP (formatted to 2 decimal places)
* Discount percent
* Amount off MSRP (formatted to 2 decimal places)
* Discounted price (formatted to 2 decimal places)
