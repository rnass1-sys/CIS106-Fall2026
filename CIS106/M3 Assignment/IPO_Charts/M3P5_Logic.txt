[INPUT]
* Radius of the circle

[PROCESSING]
1. Prompt the user for the circle radius.
2. Set PI equal to 3.14.
3. Multiply PI by the radius twice to find the area.
   Formula: area = 3.14 * radius * radius
4. Multiply 2 by PI and the radius to find the perimeter.
   Formula: perimeter = 2 * 3.14 * radius

[OUTPUT]
* Circle Area (formatted to 2 decimal places)
* Circle Perimeter (formatted to 2 decimal places)
