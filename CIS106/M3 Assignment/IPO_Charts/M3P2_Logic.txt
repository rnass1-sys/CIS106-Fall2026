[INPUT]
* Student last name
* Midterm exam score
* Final exam score

[PROCESSING]
1. Prompt the user for name, midterm score, and final score.
2. Take 40% of the midterm score and add it to 60% of the final score.
   Formula: total_points = (midterm_score * 0.40) + (final_score * 0.60)

[OUTPUT]
* Student last name
* Total exam points (formatted to 1 decimal place)
